## Workflow Steps

1. Frame the question and define explicit eligibility criteria, outcomes and review scope before screening.
2. Build concept blocks with MeSH and free-text synonyms. Combine blocks and test retrieval against known eligible papers. Save the exact query, filters, search date and retrieval count.
3. Export identifiers and metadata. Deduplicate by PMID or DOI and reconcile probable duplicates manually. Screen titles and abstracts, then full texts, recording exclusions and reasons.
4. Extract design, population, denominators, outcomes, effect estimates and uncertainty. Record the source location for key claims and mark records with abstract-only access.
5. Verify PMIDs, DOIs, bibliographic details and correction or retraction status. Synthesize findings by question and describe bias, heterogeneity and evidence gaps.

## Statistical/Validation Checklist

- Reconcile retrieved, deduplicated, screened, excluded and included records.
- Check for overlapping cohorts, inconsistent outcome definitions and incompatible effect measures before pooling.
- Verify that the cited source supports the exact claim and study population. Document any second-reviewer or discrepancy-resolution process used.

## Expected Artifacts

- Reproducible search log, screening ledger, extraction table and citation-checked synthesis.
- Selection counts for a flow diagram when needed.

## Common Pitfalls

- Relying on MeSH alone for recently indexed work or applying narrow filters before testing recall.
- Counting multiple reports from the same cohort as independent evidence.
